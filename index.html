The partner earning opportunity

What you could earn.

Four distinct opportunities.
Each has its own eligibility and approval requirements.

US$500

POS Pro referral bonus

A one-time payout for a qualifying POS Pro referral.

  • Submit the Shopify POS lead form in your Partner Dashboard.
  • Within 6 months of referral, the merchant must be new to POS Pro and activate it, transact on 10+ days per month for 2 months, and generate more than US$1,000 GMV per month.

First payment within 30 days after all terms are met.

Referral bonus terms ↗

20%

POS Payments profit share

20% of Shopify POS Payments profit—not 20% of merchant sales, GMV, or POS Pro subscription fees.

  • Submit the Shopify POS lead form in your Partner Dashboard.
  • The merchant must be new to Shopify Payments on POS and process more than US$1,000 Gross Payments Volume through it.
  • Available in eligible merchant locations listed in Shopify’s terms.

Paid monthly; a 4-year term starts with the first partner payment. First payment within 30 days after all terms are met.

Profit-share terms ↗

US$900

BYOP launch payout

A separate payout to an eligible partner for completing the included POS launch services.

  • Merchant accepts the TOS, commits to POS Pro for one year, and submits the BYOP form with your help.
  • Deliver the included launch scope and confirm the merchant is live.
  • Email pos.partner.launch@shopify.com to initiate payout.

Payout is subject to eligibility confirmation and any missing-information checks.

See the included launch scope ↓

+ Marketing funds

Partner marketing support

Potential MDF support for your industry-specific outbound campaign, if approved by Shopify’s marketing team.

  • Submit your campaign plan and funding request for review.

Confirm approval and funding terms before committing spend.

The partner payout path

Three steps. One clear handoff.

Form first. Launch next.
Completion email starts the payout process.

01

Merchant + partner

Confirm eligibility

The merchant accepts the POS Pro yearly / Retail Subscription Terms in Shopify Admin. The partner must be enrolled in the Shopify Partner Program.

Confirm the launch or migration scope and BYOP eligibility with Shopify before making commitments.

02

Merchant submits · Partner assists

Complete the BYOP form together

Once the merchant has accepted the Retail Subscription Terms (TOS) and committed to POS Pro for one year, help them complete and submit the BYOP form. Review all merchant and partner details together before submission.

Verify the Shop ID, myshopify.com URL, and partner organization ID are accurate and consistent across the submission and payout request.

Open the merchant form ↗
03

Partner

Launch, then request payout

Complete the agreed POS launch or migration and confirm the merchant is live.

Email pos.partner.launch@shopify.com with the accurate identifiers, completion date, and a short services summary to initiate the payout process.

!

Submitting the form does not trigger payout. The partner must email after launch completion. Payout remains subject to eligibility confirmation and any missing-information checks.

The US$900 launch scope

What Shopify expects you to deliver.

The eligible BYOP payout covers these launch services.
Agree on any additional work separately with merchants.

✓ Included partner launch services

  • Introductory meeting

    Initial contact and an introductory call between the partner and the Shopify Retail merchant.

  • Core data migration

    Migrate Products, Customers, and Gift Cards from the existing POS to Shopify POS. Migration is limited to these defined data elements.

  • Set-up audit

    Audit system settings and configuration, and confirm the hardware set-up. This is an audit—not step-by-step hardware installation.

  • Go-live and post-launch support

    Complete a test transaction when the merchant is ready to go live, confirm the system is functional and transacting, and provide 5 days of post-launch support for launch-related items.

× Not included

  • Historical data migration
  • Step-by-step hardware set-up
  • Staff training

These services are outside the included launch scope. If offered, agree the additional scope and fees directly with merchants.
